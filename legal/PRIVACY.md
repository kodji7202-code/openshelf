## 2. What the Site processes

The Site is a static website. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser for tracking, and
loads no analytics, advertising or third-party script.

| Processing | Data | Purpose | Legal basis | Retention |

**Hosting.** The Site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). GitHub receives the

outside the EEA relies on `[mechanism: standard contractual clauses / adequacy decision]`.

**No decision with legal effect** is taken about you by automated means, and no profiling is done.

## 3. What is published

A published entry shows the display name and GitHub handle you provided, the link to the public
OpenBot template, and the description you wrote. It never publishes your email address. Your GitHub
handle is public by nature and is linked to your GitHub profile.

## 4. Recipients

## 5. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability, and
the right to withdraw consent where processing rests on it, without affecting processing already done.
Write to `[contact e-mail]`. You may also complain to a supervisory authority; in Romania that is the
ANSPDCP (https://www.anspdcp.ro/), and you may complain in the country where you live or work.

## 6. Children

The Site is not addressed to children. It follows the age threshold of the platform it links to
(16 years), and asks for no data that would identify a child.

## 7. Changes

A change to this policy is a pull request; the repository history is the record. The date of the last
change appears in the commit that touched this file.

## 8. What this policy does not cover

Following a link to `openbot.run` or to GitHub puts you under that service's own privacy policy.
OpenShelf does not control, and is not responsible for, what those services process.

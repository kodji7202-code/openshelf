# Legal drafts

Both files here are **drafts adapted from the legal verdict of 2026-10-27**. They are not in force:
they do not name an operator, an address, a contact, a jurisdiction or a retention period, and they
have not been reviewed by a lawyer.

- [`TERMS.md`](TERMS.md) — terms of use, licensing of contributions, moderation and notice-and-action.
- [`PRIVACY.md`](PRIVACY.md) — what is processed, why, for how long, and the rights of a visitor.

Before the catalogue is announced, these must be completed:

1. Decide the operator (a natural person or a company) and put its legal name, form, registration
   number, address and contact in both files.
2. Fill every `[placeholder]`: hosting retention, hosting transfer mechanism, report address, response
   time, jurisdiction, email retention.
3. Have a lawyer read both, with attention to the DSA notice-and-action clause and to the fact that
   the catalogue publishes GitHub handles.
4. Only then link them from the site footer as terms in force. Today the footer links the draft and
   says so.

What is already true, and is what the drafts describe: no cookies, no analytics, no accounts on the
site, votes read from GitHub reactions at build time, hosting on GitHub Pages, submissions through
GitHub under the submitter's own account.
